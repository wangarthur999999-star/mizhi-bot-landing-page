Questions Business Owners Ask

Honest answers. No sales talk.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp number? ▼
Yes. We connect to your current WhatsApp number. Your customers keep messaging the same number — they just get faster, better replies.
What if the bot gives a wrong answer? ▼
We hard-code your prices, hours, and policies. The bot cannot make up prices or offer discounts you didn't approve. For anything it's unsure about, it says "let me check with the team" and notifies you — rather than guessing.
Can I jump in and take over a conversation? ▼
Absolutely. The bot handles routine questions, but when a conversation needs a human — or you just want to step in — you can take over directly from your WhatsApp. The customer won't know the difference.
Is my WhatsApp account at risk of being banned? ▼
We use WhatsApp's official Business API where possible. For businesses that prefer the standard WhatsApp app, we use a connection method that mimics normal phone usage. We're transparent about this — ask us and we'll explain the trade-offs for your specific situation.
What if I don't like it? ▼
Cancel anytime. No contract, no penalty. Your first 14 days are free — if it's not saving you time by day 10, cancel and you owe nothing.
Do I need to install anything? ▼
No. You keep using WhatsApp like always. We handle all the setup remotely. The only thing you need to do is scan a QR code during onboarding — that takes 30 seconds.
