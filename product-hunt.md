## Description

mizhi-bot is an AI customer service agent that plugs into your WhatsApp and Telegram. It auto-replies to customer questions, schedules appointments (synced to Google Calendar), scores leads, and runs follow-up campaigns — all on autopilot.

**What makes it different:**
- **No setup wizard nonsense.** Tell us about your business, we ship you a configured bot. Live in one afternoon.
- **Actually multilingual.** Auto-detects Chinese, English, Dutch, Spanish — no configuration needed.
- **Doesn't hallucinate your prices.** Guardrails keep it on-brand. No medical claims, no fake discounts.
- **Built for small service businesses.** Clinics, salons, gyms, barbershops — if you book time slots, it works.

**Tech:** Baileys (WhatsApp Web protocol, no Business API needed) + DeepSeek AI + Google Calendar sync.

**Pricing:** $49/month. 7-day free trial. No credit card required.

**Maker:** Solo dev in Paramaribo, Suriname. Built this after watching my friend's beauty clinic lose bookings every night because no one was awake to reply on WhatsApp.
